Safety Tips

Verify the receiver

Check the receiver name and UPI ID before confirming a payment.

Protect your PIN and OTP

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN, or passwords with anyone.

Do not trust unknown requests

Be careful with unexpected collect requests, refund offers, prizes, and urgent payment demands.

Check links and messages

Avoid opening suspicious links or entering payment credentials on unknown pages.

Keep apps updated

Use official app stores and keep your device and payment apps updated.

Report quickly

If a real payment fraud occurs, contact your bank or payment provider through official channels as soon as possible.

Emergency Help

If you believe a real transaction is fraudulent, act quickly.

1. Stop further payments

Do not send additional money to the suspicious receiver.

2. Contact the bank or payment provider

Use the official app, website, or customer-care channel.

3. Secure your account

Change credentials if needed and review recent transactions.

4. Preserve evidence

Keep transaction references, screenshots, and relevant messages for reporting.

This section is general educational guidance for the college prototype. For a real incident, follow the official instructions of your bank, payment provider, and relevant authorities.
